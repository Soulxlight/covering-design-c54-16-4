audit while qualified external review was unavailable. Project derivations,
review and packaging used AI assistance. Mopî prepared this exposition,
the independent 222 coefficient rebuild, extended public verification,
tests and handoff. The Pētā investigator supplied the analytic 223 route
and support graphs. The scout investigator supplied the 975-variable

the 19-block avoidance cap and star envelope used by Pētā, and the scout's
